Music

I’ve been a musician my entire life; from winding up my father at age 5 with a small purple electronic keyboard at 6am thinking I’m the next Chopin, to winding up my family, friends and neighbours with various sounds to this day.

I’ve played all sorts of instruments throughout my life that it might be easier to name what I can’t play/haven’t played. It’s one of my life’s missions to learn and play as many instruments as possible.

Nowadays my main focus is Guitar, Bass, Drums, and I’m slowly teaching myself Clarinet and Banjo.

I currently have a few music projects on the go:

  • Rosie (aka Lexwell) is music I both write and arrange for fingerstyle guitar and various other acoustic instruments.
  • Maybe Manitoba is more of a rock/punk type sound.
