(be)longing’s Debut and More Fingerstyle Porter Robinson! - Live @ Eon Night FINALE, December 2021

Rosie · (be)longing live (Rosie @ Eon Night Dec 2021)

The set list:

  • Home Away from Home - (be)longing live intro version
  • Divinity (orig by Porter Robinson, a new arrangement!)
  • Innocence (orig by Madeon)
  • Sweet Time (orig by Porter Robinson)
  • Zonsopkomst
  • Wind Tempos (orig by Porter Robinson, Nurture Live Secret Sky edit)
  • Blossom (orig by Porter Robinson, Nurture Live Secret Sky edit)
  • Goodbye to a World (orig by Porter Robinson, Nurture Live Secret Sky edit)
  • Sweetest Dreams (Rêves les Plus Doux) (ft. Vaida on vocals)

As the pandemic lockdown dragged on, I found myself beginning to write some music for fingerstyle guitar; in part inspired by arrangement work I was doing on Worlds Live Acoustic, as well as just where my head was at that time.

The early days of the pandemic were for me a time of change and rediscovering who I am. I named the collection of songs I was writing (be)longing to describe my emotions at the time; the idea of longing to belong.

I was asked in late 2021 if I would like to play a 30 minute-long set for Eon Night FINALE, the farewell of the community live music event night that had been running in the Porter Robinson Discord server for a number of years. Since the Worlds Live Acoustic set I had done previously was so well received, absolutely I said yes!

I decided this would be a good opportunity to debut some songs from (be)longing, even if they weren’t fully finished, alongside a mix of brand new Porter Robinson arrangements (including a heavily requested arrangement of Blossom!). I felt they complimented each other so well since my love of Porter Robinson’s music (especially the Nurture era) heavily inspired my songwriting at the time, and so I thought an audience of Porter Robinson fans would enjoy it!

A finished release of (be)longing will see the light of day as an EP one day. I’ve also continued working on improving and arranging more Nurture-era (and beyond!) stuff that I would love to perform like this again!

But for now, gn Eon Night </3
